Risk Disclosure

ZipAsset presents EV charger models and related project information. Charger purchases and deployments can involve technical, site, operational, commercial, and regulatory risks. Any investment-related illustrations on the website are not live offerings or verified performance.

Effective date: July 23, 2026 Last updated: October 29, 2026 Applies to website information and enquiries

1. Illustrative information only

Any investment, yield, return, lock-period, utilization, or cash-flow figures visible on the website are illustrative frontend content only. They are not verified performance, a current offer, or a promise of future returns. The website is not connected to investment onboarding, payment, asset-management, or portfolio reporting systems.

2. Asset and Deployment Risk

  • Charger compatibility and suitability depend on the vehicle, site, power supply, and project requirements.
  • Site readiness, electrical capacity, permissions, installation scope, and contractor availability may affect cost and timing.
  • Equipment may experience faults, downtime, maintenance needs, or performance limitations.
  • Specifications, availability, warranties, installation, and after-sales service must be confirmed in writing for each purchase.

3. Demand and Utilization Risk

Actual charger use and project economics can differ from expectations because of customer demand, seasonality, site access, nearby alternatives, energy costs, tariffs, and changes in vehicle or charging behavior.

4. Regulatory and Policy Risk

Applicable electrical, safety, building, land-use, transport, tax, and other requirements vary by location and may change. Customers and project operators should confirm requirements with the relevant authorities and qualified professionals.

5. Liquidity and Exit Risk

A purchase enquiry does not guarantee a transaction, delivery date, project completion, or commercial outcome. Any separate investment offering, if made in the future, would carry its own liquidity and exit risks and require offering-specific documentation.

6. Counterparty and Operational Risk

Project delivery may rely on installers, site hosts, utilities, vendors, or other service providers. Delays or failures by a counterparty may affect cost, operation, maintenance, or delivery.

7. Concentration Risk

A project concentrated in a single site, region, charger model, customer, or supplier may be more exposed to disruptions affecting that specific dependency.

8. Independent review

Obtain project-specific technical, electrical, legal, tax, and commercial advice as appropriate before ordering equipment or entering a deployment arrangement. Do not rely on website information as a substitute for that review.

KYC / AML Notice

This notice explains the status of identity and financial-crime checks in relation to the ZipAsset website. The website currently supports charger and franchise enquiries; it does not provide an investment onboarding or payment flow and does not request KYC or source-of-funds documents through its general enquiry form.

Effective date: July 23, 2026 Last updated: October 29, 2026 Current website: charger and franchise enquiries

1. Do not send sensitive documents through the enquiry form

Do not submit identity documents, bank statements, payment details, passwords, or source-of-funds information through the general website enquiry form or by unsolicited email. Contact Zipbolt first if a project-specific process requires additional information, and confirm the appropriate secure channel.

2. No investment onboarding on this website

The website is not connected to investment onboarding, KYC collection, payment processing, or portfolio management. Submitting a product or franchise enquiry does not create an investment account or require the submission of source-of-funds or source-of-wealth information.

3. Separate legal or commercial requirements

A future product, transaction, or business relationship may be subject to identity checks or other legal and compliance requirements. Any such process would be described separately, including what information is required, why it is required, and how to submit it.

4. Contact

For questions about this notice, contact Zipbolt Innovations Private Limited at info@zip-bolt.com.

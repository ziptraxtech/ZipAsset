Investor Eligibility & Availability

ZipAsset currently presents charger and franchise information and does not provide a live investment offering through this website. Product availability and purchase terms must be confirmed directly with Zipbolt.

Effective date: July 23, 2026 Last updated: October 29, 2026 Any future offering would have separate terms

1. Current website

  • Visitors may browse the website and submit a charger or franchise enquiry.
  • An enquiry is not an order, reservation, investment, or confirmation that a product or project is available.
  • Pricing, specifications, delivery, installation, service scope, and commercial terms require separate confirmation.

2. Location and product availability

Product availability and the ability to supply, install, or support equipment can depend on location, site conditions, applicable requirements, logistics, and project scope. Contact Zipbolt to confirm whether a particular request can be served.

3. No live investment offering

The website is not connected to investment onboarding, KYC, payment, asset-management, or portfolio systems. Illustrative investment-related content does not establish an available offer, eligibility, or investor rights.

4. Any future offering

If Zipbolt presents an investment or other regulated product in the future, access would be subject to separate offering documents, eligibility criteria, investor rights, risk disclosures, and applicable legal requirements. Nothing on this page establishes that such an offering exists.

5. Contact

For product availability and project enquiries, contact info@zip-bolt.com.

This page is general website information, not legal advice or confirmation that an investment product is available.

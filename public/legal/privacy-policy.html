Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Zipbolt Innovations Private Limited handles information submitted through the ZipAsset website, including charger and franchise enquiries.

Effective date: July 23, 2026 Last updated: October 29, 2026 Controller: Zipbolt Innovations Pvt Ltd Applies to website visitors and enquirers

1. Information We Collect

  • Details you submit in an enquiry, such as your name, email, phone number, company, charger or franchise interest, use case, quantity, deployment location, timeline, preferred contact method, and message.
  • Referral and campaign details that may be included with your enquiry, such as the page you came from or campaign parameters.
  • Technical and usage information processed through website analytics and security services, which may include device, browser, and page interaction information.
  • Information you choose to provide when contacting us by email or another channel.

The general enquiry form is not intended for identity documents, payment details, passwords, or other sensitive personal information. The website does not currently collect investment KYC or AML documents through an investment onboarding flow.

2. Why We Use Information

  • To operate the website and respond to inquiries.
  • To respond to product, charger, and franchise enquiries and discuss the project details you provide.
  • To operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the website and enquiry process.
  • To understand website usage through analytics and assess the effectiveness of communications or campaigns.
  • To meet legal obligations, protect our rights, or address misuse where appropriate.

3. Sharing of Information

We may share relevant information with personnel handling your enquiry and with service providers who support website hosting, email delivery, analytics, authentication (if enabled), security, and business operations. We may also disclose information where required by law or needed to protect rights and safety. We do not describe the current website as operating an investment payment or KYC onboarding service.

4. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to and manage an enquiry, maintain appropriate business records, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the website. Retention periods depend on the information and the reason it was collected.

5. Cookies and Analytics

The website uses Vercel Analytics and may use technical storage or services needed to operate the site and optional sign-in functionality. Third-party providers may process information under their own privacy terms. Browser settings can control some storage, though disabling it may affect site features.

6. Security

We use safeguards appropriate to the information and services involved. No online service or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Please do not send sensitive identity or payment information through the general enquiry form.

7. Your Choices

You may contact us to ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you submitted, subject to applicable law and records we may need to retain. You may also contact us with questions about this Policy.

8. Contact

Privacy-related requests may be sent to info@zip-bolt.com. Zipbolt Innovations Private Limited is located at UG-006 Ground Floor, MGF Metropolis Mall, Mehrauli-Gurgaon Road, Gurugram, Haryana 122002, India.
